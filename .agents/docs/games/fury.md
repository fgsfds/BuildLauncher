# Ion Fury addon preparation

Read the [common preparation workflow](../addon-conversion-plan.md),
[manifest contract](../addon.json-Specifications.md) and
[common artwork rules](../addon-artwork-style.md) first. This profile applies to
`game.name: Fury`; its recipes are not defaults for other games.

## Resource and runtime boundaries

CON and native GRP/ART are candidates under Fury-capable ports. Trace the active
Fury scripts/resources and any expansion requirement. Do not copy Duke episode
menu edits, stock GAME.CON or original Duke palettes into Fury. Use Fury DEF
entry points and its verified palette; Blood RFF/INI/DAT rules do not apply.
